What each row contains

  • Company name, company number and a link to its Companies House page.
  • Incorporation date, company type and activity (SIC) codes.
  • Registered office address, post town and postcode.
  • A category: landlord or property investment; letting or managing agent; block or residents' management company.
  • A flag where three or more companies share one registered office, which usually means an accountant's or formation agent's address.

Company-level data only. No directors' names or personal details are included.

Things to know before you buy

  • "City" means the postcode area, so Sheffield includes Rotherham and Barnsley addresses, and Manchester includes Salford and Stockport.
  • A registered office can be an accountant's address, not the property or the owner's home.
  • A new company shows an intention to hold or let property. It is not proof of a purchase.
  • Partnerships (LLPs and LPs) carry no activity code on the register, so they are not included.
