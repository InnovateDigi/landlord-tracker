Privacy

Privacy notice

What we do with your details if you sign up for reminders, in plain English.

Last updated 3 October 2026.

Who we are

We are Innovate Digital, a small independent business. We run the Landlord Compliance Tracker and these guides. We are not affiliated with Innovate UK or UKRI.

We decide how your details are used. To contact us, email hello@innovateuk.uk.

What we collect

You do not have to give us anything to read the guides. We collect your details only if you sign up for reminders.

If you sign up, we collect two things:

  • your email address
  • the region you choose

We do not ask for your name, your address or your phone number.

Why we collect it

We use your email address to send you what you asked for. That is an email when registration opens for your region, an email when the rules change, and occasional news about our tracker.

We use your region to decide which reminders to send you. We rely on your consent. You can take it back at any time.

Where your details are held

Your details are held by Kit, the email service we use. Kit stores our list and sends our emails. Kit's privacy policy explains how Kit handles personal data.

When you press the sign-up button, your browser sends your email address and region straight to Kit. Kit then shows you its own page. We do not control that page.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until you ask us to delete them. If you only unsubscribe, Kit keeps your record, marked as unsubscribed.

How to unsubscribe

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Click it and our emails stop. You can also email hello@innovateuk.uk and we will do it for you.

How to ask us to delete your details

Email hello@innovateuk.uk. Tell us the email address you signed up with. We will delete your email address and region from Kit and reply to confirm.

Your rights

You can ask us what details we hold about you. You can ask us to correct them or delete them. Email hello@innovateuk.uk.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your details, please tell us first. The ICO recommends raising your concern with the organisation first. If you are still unhappy, you can then complain to the ICO.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. It has no analytics, no advertising and no social media buttons. It loads no scripts, fonts or images from other websites.

GitHub hosts these pages. GitHub's documentation says it logs the IP address of visitors to GitHub Pages sites, for security. We do not use that information.

The page Kit shows after you sign up is Kit's, not ours. Kit's privacy policy explains what Kit does.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will change the date at the top of the page.
