PRS Database dates by region: when to register in England

Your deadline depends on where the rental property is, not where you live. Find your region, then find your date.

Facts last checked 3 October 2026 against GOV.UK and legislation.gov.uk.

What is still draft

  • The rules in this guide come from the draft Private Rented Sector Database Regulations 2026. They were laid for approval by both Houses of Parliament. legislation.gov.uk says they have not yet been made as a UK Statutory Instrument.
  • The start dates are in the draft regulations. The register-by deadlines come from Government guidance on the Housing Hub.
  • The Act sections that create the registration duty and the penalties are not yet in force. The Government's explanatory memorandum says Commencement No. 4 Regulations will bring the database provisions into force. We could not find those regulations on legislation.gov.uk on 3 October 2026.
  • The council lists for each region are in the draft regulations and could change.
  • Dates and details could change before the regulations are made. Check GOV.UK before you act.

"Rule starts" is the date the draft regulations come into force for properties in that region. It is the date from which you are legally required to register. The Housing Hub calls the register-by date the deadline to register.

  • Once your region's rule starts, you have a three-month window to register. After the deadline, councils in that region can start enforcement.
  • You can register early. You can register whenever you want from 15 December 2026, but before your property's regional deadline.
  • The Government says everyone actively letting property needs to be registered by 14 November 2027.
  • The fee is pro-rated during the rollout so that people who sign up first do not pay more.

How to tell which region a property is in

  1. Find the property's council. Enter its postcode in GOV.UK's Find your local council tool.
  2. Note every council name shown. Many parts of England have two tiers, a county council and a district, borough or city council. For the postcode CV21 2LS, the tool shows Rugby Borough Council and Warwickshire County Council.
  3. Look for either name in the lists below. The draft regulations define each region by named councils, as they existed on 15 December 2026. Warwickshire County Council is in the West Midlands list.
  4. Use that region's dates from the table above.

County councils are responsible for services across the whole of a county. If you live in London but your rental is in Birmingham, the West Midlands deadline applies.

Questions

What are the PRS Database dates for each region?

West Midlands: the rule starts 15 December 2026 and you register by 14 March 2027. East of England: the rule starts 15 January 2027 and you register by 14 April 2027. East Midlands: the rule starts 15 February 2027 and you register by 14 May 2027. South East: the rule starts 15 March 2027 and you register by 14 June 2027. Yorkshire and Humber: the rule starts 15 April 2027 and you register by 14 July 2027. North West: the rule starts 15 May 2027 and you register by 14 August 2027. North East: the rule starts 15 June 2027 and you register by 14 September 2027. London: the rule starts 15 July 2027 and you register by 14 October 2027. South West: the rule starts 15 August 2027 and you register by 14 November 2027. The start dates are in the draft regulations and the register-by deadlines are in Government guidance.

Which date applies if I live in a different region from my property?

The property's location decides it. If you live in London but your rental property is in Birmingham, you must meet the West Midlands deadline.

How long do I have to register once my region starts?

You have a three-month window. After that deadline, councils in the region can start enforcement.

Can I register before my region's date?

Yes. Government guidance says you can register your properties whenever you want from 15 December 2026, but before the registration deadline for your property's area.

How do I find out which region my property is in?

Use GOV.UK's Find your local council tool with the property's postcode, then look for the council names in the draft regulations' list for each region.

When must all landlords be registered by?

The Government says all landlords actively letting property will need to have registered by 14 November 2027.
