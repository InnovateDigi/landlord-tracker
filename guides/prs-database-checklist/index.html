PRS Database checklist: what to have ready

Tick off each item before you register. The list follows the draft regulations and Government guidance.

Facts last checked 3 October 2026 against GOV.UK and legislation.gov.uk.

What is still draft

  • The rules in this guide come from the draft Private Rented Sector Database Regulations 2026. They were laid for approval by both Houses of Parliament. legislation.gov.uk says they have not yet been made as a UK Statutory Instrument.
  • Government guidance for letting agents and property managers is still to be published.
  • The lists below follow draft Schedules 2 and 3 and the Housing Hub. The final lists could change.
  • Dates and details could change before the regulations are made. Check GOV.UK before you act.

Tick each item before you register. Copy sections D to F for each property you register.

A. Before you start

  • Work out which region each property is in and note its register-by date. See dates by region.
  • Set up a GOV.UK One Login. The service needs one.
  • Offline registration routes will be available if you cannot register online.
  • Plan to pay the fee: £65 per property per year, from Government guidance.
  • If an agent manages the property, agree who supplies what. You still start the registration.

B. About you: individual landlords Landlord entry

  • Name
  • Date of birth
  • Residential address
  • Telephone number
  • Email address

Joint landlords each make their own landlord entry, but share one dwelling entry.

C. About the landlord: companies and other organisations Landlord entry

  • Name of the organisation, or the name it is known by
  • Type of legal entity
  • Address (not a PO box)
  • Telephone number and email address
  • Name, date of birth, email address and telephone number of the person making the entry for the organisation
  • Name, email address and telephone number of a nominated contact for the database
  • Companies House registration number, if it has one
  • Charity number, if applicable
  • If there is no Companies House number: names, dates of birth and addresses of all directors, trustees, partners or members of the governing body
  • If the organisation is a trust: name, date of birth, address, email address and telephone number of the lead trustee

C2. Only if someone else makes the entry for the landlord Landlord entry

Examples of who may act for a landlord:

  • a donee of a power of attorney
  • a personal representative of a deceased person's estate
  • a court-appointed deputy
  • a guardian
  • a receiver
  • a trustee in bankruptcy
  • a liquidator or provisional liquidator
  • an administrator
  • an administrative receiver
  • a mortgagee or a person appointed by the mortgagee
  • The capacity in which they act
  • Their name, address, telephone number and email address
  • A copy of a certified or sealed copy of the evidence of their authority Copy. Examples: power of attorney, grant of probate, letters of administration, deed or instrument of appointment, mortgage deed, court order or bankruptcy order.

D. Each property: the property Dwelling entry

  • Address of the property
  • The landlord's name, a correspondence address in England or Wales (not a PO box) and an email address for this property
  • Ownership type
  • Dwelling type, such as a detached, semi-detached or terraced house, or a flat
  • Number of bedrooms
  • Whether the property is currently let
  • Name and email address of the freeholder, any other superior landlord and the property manager

E. Each property: the tenancy Dwelling entry

  • Number of occupants
  • Number of households
  • Whether it needs an HMO licence, an additional licence or a selective licence, and the licence numbers if available
  • Rent charged and how often it is paid
  • Whether the rent includes utilities, and which
  • Whether it is let furnished, partly furnished or unfurnished

F. Each property: safety and energy documents Dwelling entry

  • Gas safety record, if the property has a gas supply: a copy, if one exists Copy, and the issue date
  • Electrical report: a copy of the EICR, if one exists, or the EIC Copy, and the EICR expiry date or the EIC issue date. The EICR expiry date is the date by which the next inspection and test must be carried out.
  • EPC: whether you had to give a valid EPC to the current tenant, and if so, a copy of the most recent one Copy
  • If the most recent EPC is no longer valid: the date the current tenancy started
  • If the most recent EPC rating is below the minimum level of energy efficiency: whether there is a registered MEES exemption, and which types apply

G. After you register

  • If a let property was registered before the tenancy and safety information was added, add it within 28 days of the entry being made. Draft
  • If a property was empty when you registered, add the tenancy information within 28 days of the tenancy starting. Draft
  • Update your landlord entry and each dwelling entry within 28 days of anything going out of date. Draft
  • Diary your renewal date. Entries last 12 months and the fee is paid again to renew. Draft
  • If you register more than one property, the Government says the renewal dates will be synchronised.

Questions

What do I need to register a rental property on the PRS Database?

You need a GOV.UK One Login and your own details as landlord. You also need details of each property and tenancy, and a gas safety record, electrical report and EPC where they apply. The full list is on this page.

Which documents do I upload for each property?

You upload a copy of the gas safety record if there is a gas supply. You also upload a copy of the EICR or EIC, and a copy of the most recent EPC where one was required.

What if my EPC has expired?

If the most recent EPC for the dwelling is no longer valid, you give the date the current tenancy started.

What extra information do companies and trusts need to give?

Companies and other organisations give details of the person making the entry and a nominated contact. They add a Companies House or charity number if they have one. Without a Companies House number, they list directors, trustees, partners or members. A trust also names a lead trustee.

Do I need to have everything ready on day one?

Under the draft regulations, for a let property you can make the dwelling entry first. The tenancy and safety information must then follow within 28 days.
