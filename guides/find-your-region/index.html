Find your region: PRS Database dates by council

Type your council's name to see which region it is in, when the rule starts and when to register by.

Facts last checked 3 October 2026 against GOV.UK and legislation.gov.uk.

What is still draft

  • The rules in this guide come from the draft Private Rented Sector Database Regulations 2026. They were laid for approval by both Houses of Parliament. legislation.gov.uk says they have not yet been made as a UK Statutory Instrument.
  • The start dates are in the draft regulations. The register-by deadlines come from Government guidance on the Housing Hub.
  • The Act sections that create the registration duty and the penalties are not yet in force. The Government's explanatory memorandum says Commencement No. 4 Regulations will bring the database provisions into force. We could not find those regulations on legislation.gov.uk on 3 October 2026.
  • The council names are copied from Schedule 1 to the draft regulations and could change.
  • Dates and details could change before the regulations are made. Check GOV.UK before you act.

"Rule starts" is the date the draft regulations come into force for properties in that region. It is the date from which you are legally required to register. The register-by dates come from Government guidance.

If your council is not in the table

Many parts of England have two tiers of local government. A property can have both a county council and a district, borough or city council.

In those areas the table names the county council. For example, it names Warwickshire County Council but not Rugby Borough Council. If you cannot find your council, look for your county council.

The councils are listed as they existed on 15 December 2026.

Using a postcode

  1. Find the property's council. Enter its postcode in GOV.UK's Find your local council tool.
  2. Note every council name shown. The tool can show two. For the postcode CV21 2LS it shows Rugby Borough Council and Warwickshire County Council.
  3. Look for either name in the table above. Use the dates in that row.
  4. Read the guide for that region. Each region has its own guide, linked from the table and from the list below.

Questions

How do I find out which region my rental property is in?

Enter the property's postcode in GOV.UK's Find your local council tool. Then look for the council names it shows in the table on this page. The draft regulations define each region by named councils.

Why is my district council not in the table?

Many parts of England have two tiers of local government. In those areas the table names the county council. For example, it names Warwickshire County Council but not Rugby Borough Council. Look for your county council.

Which date applies if I live in a different region from my property?

The property's location decides it. If you live in London but your rental property is in Birmingham, you must meet the West Midlands deadline.

Where do the council names come from?

They are copied from Schedule 1 to the draft regulations, which define each region by named councils. The draft could change.
