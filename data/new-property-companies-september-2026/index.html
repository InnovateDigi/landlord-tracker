New property companies by city, September 2026

1,746 companies registered in September 2026 declared a property letting, investment or management activity code. Their registered offices are in the postcode areas of 25 English cities. London is not one of them.

Data downloaded from Companies House on 2 October 2026. Counts rebuilt from the source files on 3 October 2026. Counts only: no company names and no people.

What the numbers show

These are counts of companies with the activity codes, not counts of landlords or rental properties. One month of data cannot show a trend, so we do not claim one.

29.2%

Two cities hold just under three in ten

Birmingham has 280 companies and Manchester has 230. Together that is 510 of the 1,746.

No other city has more than 99. The smallest, Lincoln, has 15. These are counts, not rates.

93.1%

More than nine in ten are in the letting or investment group

1,625 companies declared 68209 (letting or operating own or leased property) or 68100 (buying and selling own property). Their names do not look like block or residents' companies.

115 (6.6%) declared the management code, 68320, and neither of those. 6 (0.3%) have block or residents' management names.

6.6%

About one in 15 is at a shared registered office

115 companies give a registered office that three or more companies in the same city list share. They sit at 31 addresses. The largest group is 8 companies.

Manchester has the most, 32 (13.9% of its list). Ten of the 25 cities have none. Shared addresses can belong to accountants, company formation agents or serviced offices. We did not check each one.

The management-only count is also 115. That is a coincidence: the two are counted separately.

40 of 86

A city count covers a postcode area

We matched companies to cities by the start of the registered office postcode, not by city boundary. In Sheffield (area S), only 40 of 86 give Sheffield as the town in their address. Rotherham (22), Barnsley (10) and Chesterfield (6) come next.

In Birmingham (area B) the figure is 173 of 280. In Manchester (area M) it is 181 of 230.

The ten cities with the most companies

The ten cities shown have 1,190 of the 1,746 companies (68.2%).

Leeds and Newcastle upon Tyne both have 85; Bristol and Liverpool both have 73. Cities with the same count are listed A to Z.

The bars count all companies, whatever their activity code.

How we counted

Source

The Companies House public register, read through the Companies House API. We downloaded the data on 2 October 2026. Every figure on this page was recounted from our city files by script on 3 October 2026.

Which companies

Companies incorporated from 1 to 30 September 2026 that were active on the download date and declared at least one of three activity codes (SIC 2007). A company can declare more than one.

  • 68209: Other letting and operating of own or leased real estate. Declared by 1,369 of the 1,746 companies.
  • 68100: Buying and selling of own real estate. Declared by 797 of the 1,746 companies.
  • 68320: Management of real estate on a fee or contract basis. Declared by 330 of the 1,746 companies.

1,737 are private limited companies and 9 are private companies limited by guarantee.

Date range

Date of incorporation, 1 to 30 September 2026. We asked for one day at a time and checked that each day's count matched the total the API reported for that day.

How cities were matched to postcodes

A company is counted in a city when its registered office postcode starts with the letters of the city's postcode area. Examples: M for Manchester, LS for Leeds, S for Sheffield. The area is shown beside each city in the table. Postcode areas include the towns around a city, so a city count is wider than the city itself. Each company is counted once.

Letting or investment, management only, block or residents'

We sorted the companies by name and activity code. A company goes in the first group that fits.

  1. Block or residents' management. The name has a word or phrase such as residents, right to manage, freehold, leasehold or management company. Management company alone does not count if the name also shows a business such as lettings or investment.
  2. Letting or investment. The company declared 68209 or 68100.
  3. Management only. The company declared 68320 without 68209 or 68100. This group is not only agents. It includes some short-let and serviced-accommodation operators.

These rules read names and codes only. They are a guide, not a judgement about what a company does.

Shared addresses

A company is at a shared address when three or more companies in the same city list give the same registered office address (same postcode, building and street lines). The count sees only the companies in this data, so it is a minimum.

What the figures do not show

  • They count companies, not landlords, rental properties or homes. A new company with a property code is not proof that a property has been bought or let.
  • Companies choose their own activity codes and can change them later.
  • A registered office may be an accountant's, solicitor's or company formation agent's address, or the owner's home. It is not necessarily where any property is.
  • Limited liability partnerships and limited partnerships are not counted. The register holds no activity codes for them.
  • Companies outside these 25 postcode areas are not counted. London is not one of the 25 cities. Companies that were not active on the download date are not counted.
  • This is one month. We make no comparison with other months and claim no trend.
  • These are counts, not rates. We have not adjusted for the size of each area or its housing stock.
  • The register is updated daily, so counting again later can give slightly different numbers.
  • The town in an address is as typed on the register. Some entries are not towns.
  • Company-level data only. No directors, owners or other people are in this page or the CSV.
